# Dashboard visual analysis

Management dashboards show attendance, action queues and task status without changing attendance or payroll records. HR, super-admin and the assigned company CEO receive company-wide data. Managers and team leaders receive only their managed people. Employees receive only personal analysis.

The default period is today plus the preceding six days. Users may choose 30 days or a custom inclusive range of at most 31 days. Attendance status and trend points retain their exact date and status for drill-down.

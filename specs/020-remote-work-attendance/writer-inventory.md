Repository audit on 2026-10-26. This is an integration checklist, not evidence that the legacy writers already obey a remote-work guard. Do not enable the pilot until each affected writer is characterized and governed or explicitly excluded by an enforced server/rules boundary.

Reading an attendance collection is not itself evidence that a component writes it.

## Scheduler evidence and limits

The checked-in `attendance-reminders.yml` workflow is `workflow_dispatch` only. `notification-web.js` runs background attendance under the existing `background_attendance` lease, and its authenticated manual dispatch/reminder paths use that same lease. Policy activation is composed into these paths, before automatic attendance/reminders, without adding a timer or workflow. Actual deployed Hostinger/GitHub ownership has not been inspected in this continuation and remains a production gate.

Activation scans at most ten due company heads per tick, reads the chosen immutable revision and current active revision, and commits only that company's head/projection/calendar guard/audit. A failed head receives operational retry backoff if its discovered version/pointer is still current; its scheduled effective date remains unchanged. Successful or superseded heads leave the due scan. Upcoming attendance command integration still needs to resolve the effective snapshot at action time rather than relying on the scheduler's polling interval for exact-boundary authorization.
